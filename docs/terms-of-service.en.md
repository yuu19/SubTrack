## Article 3 (Overview of the Service)
The Service is a web application that allows Users to manage various subscription services in one place. Its main functions are as follows:

1. **Subscription management**
   1. Tracking external services: register and manage subscriptions such as Netflix and Spotify.
   2. Payment reminders: send notifications a specified number of days in advance.
   3. Offline support: provide limited offline access through IndexedDB caching.
2. **Plan management**
   1. Free Plan: up to 5 subscriptions can be registered.
   2. Premium Plan: unlimited registrations, CSV export, and per-subscription notification settings are available.
   3. Stripe integration: plans can be managed through the payment portal.
3. **Notification system**
   1. Push notifications: browser notifications using the Web Push API.
   2. Email notifications: emails sent through the Resend API. Depending on the notification type, content may be sent in Japanese or English.
   3. Scheduled execution: notification checks are run every hour using Cloudflare Cron.
