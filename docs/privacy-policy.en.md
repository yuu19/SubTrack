# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

## Article 1 (Purpose)
This Privacy Policy sets forth how personal information is handled in connection with the subscription management application "SubTrack" (the "Service") provided by SubTrack (the "Company"). The Company recognizes the importance of personal information and will appropriately manage and protect it in compliance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other applicable laws and regulations.

## Article 2 (Collection of Personal Information)
1. The Company may collect the following personal information when customers use the Service:
   1. Name, email address, and authentication-related information such as identifiers and access tokens for external authentication providers
   2. Payment-related information such as Stripe customer IDs and subscription IDs. Card information is managed by Stripe and is not retained by the Company.
   3. Registered subscription information such as service name, amount, and billing date
   4. Push notification subscription information such as endpoints and public keys, as well as notification settings
   5. Service usage history, cookie information, and access log information such as IP address, user agent, and request headers
   6. Any other information necessary to provide the Service
2. The Company shall collect personal information by lawful and fair means and shall not acquire it through improper means.

## Article 3 (Purpose of Use of Personal Information)
The Company uses collected personal information for the following purposes:
1. To provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service
   1. Registration and management of subscriptions and sending payment reminders
   2. Management and authentication of user accounts, including external authentication
   3. Plan management for the Free Plan and Premium Plan
   4. Payment processing via Stripe integration and plan management through the billing portal
   5. Provision of the notification system, including push notifications and email notifications, and management of subscription data related to notifications
   6. Provision of offline support features through IndexedDB caching
   7. Collection and analysis of logs for error analysis, quality improvement, and abuse prevention
2. To respond to inquiries from customers and provide support
3. To provide information, updates, and campaign notices related to the Service
4. To analyze Service usage and conduct marketing activities
5. To disclose information when required by law or by governmental authorities
6. For purposes incidental to the purposes listed above

## Article 4 (Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties)
1. The Company will not provide personal information to third parties without customer consent, except as permitted by law.
2. However, the Company may provide personal information to third parties without customer consent in the following cases:
   1. When disclosure is required by law
   2. When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property and obtaining the individual's consent is difficult
   3. When especially necessary for improving public health or promoting the sound growth of children and obtaining the individual's consent is difficult
   4. When cooperation with a national or local government body, or a party entrusted by such body, is necessary for the performance of duties prescribed by law and obtaining the individual's consent may interfere with that performance
   5. When information is provided to subcontractors. The Company may outsource all or part of the handling of personal information to the extent necessary to achieve the purpose of use, for example to Stripe, Resend, Google, Sentry, or Cloudflare. In such cases, the Company will appropriately supervise those subcontractors.

## Article 5 (Security Control Measures for Personal Information)
1. The Company implements technical and organizational security control measures to prevent leakage, loss, or damage of personal information.
   1. Access control: manage access permissions to the D1 Database appropriately
   2. Network security: use Cloudflare Workers to build a secure execution environment
   3. Encryption: encrypt communications where necessary
   4. Employee education: thoroughly educate employees regarding the handling of personal information
2. The Company will maintain internal rules relating to the protection of personal information and continuously review and improve its management structure.

## Article 6 (Disclosure, Correction, Suspension of Use, etc.)
1. Customers may request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, or erasure of their personal information from the Company.
2. If a customer wishes to make such a request, they must do so in accordance with procedures separately specified by the Company.
3. When the Company receives such a request, it will respond appropriately in accordance with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information. However, the Company may not comply if doing so would interfere with provision of the Service or violate laws and regulations.

## Article 7 (Use of Cookies and Similar Technologies)
1. The Company may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to improve convenience, analyze usage, and deliver advertising.
2. Customers may refuse to receive cookies or delete received cookies through browser settings. However, in such cases, some functions of the Service may become unavailable.

## Article 8 (Personal Information of Minors)
1. The Company gives special consideration to the handling of personal information of minors.
2. The Service is generally intended for use by adults. If a minor uses the Service, the minor shall do so with the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

## Article 9 (Changes to the Privacy Policy)
1. The Company may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time due to amendments to applicable laws such as the Act on the Protection of Personal Information or business needs.
2. If the Privacy Policy is changed, the Company will notify customers by posting it within the Service or by another appropriate method.
3. Unless otherwise specified by the Company, the revised Privacy Policy shall become effective upon posting within the Service.
