## What Belongs Here

The Trigger Map reveals the human forces behind product decisions through five workshops:

1. **Business Goals** — What the business needs to achieve
2. **Target Groups** — Who the users are
3. **Driving Forces** — What motivates and frightens each persona
4. **Prioritization** — Which forces matter most
5. **Feature Impact** — How product features address the highest-priority forces

For brownfield work, this folder may also hold a focused trigger map for the current improvement target rather than a full greenfield exercise.
